Section 01

Who We Are

Leadership in Change is a publication at the intersection of leadership and AI, covering ethical AI use, change management, guardrails, and strategies that help leaders create sustained impact in the age of AI. Our audience includes CEOs, entrepreneurs, executives, and leaders across industries.

Section 02

What We're Looking For

  • Roughly 1,000 to 1,500 words.
  • Interactive or multimedia content encouraged (embedded videos, demos, diagrams, screenshots).
  • Actionable content: include at least one concrete framework, checklist, or step-by-step action a leader can apply this week.
  • Always define technical terms. This is a nontechnical audience. Write for leaders, not engineers.
  • Serious, forward-thinking tone.

A note on AI. I'm all for AI assistance, use the tools. But I reserve the right to decline content that reads as fully AI-generated. Your voice, your experience, your point of view are what make a post worth publishing.

Section 03

How to Structure Your Post

  1. Brief intro about yourself. Who you are, what you do, why this topic matters to you. (2 to 3 sentences. Joel will write the publication intro and outro.)
  2. Your content (roughly 1,000 to 1,500 words).
  3. Your CTA. You're welcome to include a call to action and links to your own articles or work.

Section 04

Visuals

This is your stage. Bring your full style so readers who resonate with your work can find you and follow. Use whatever formats best showcase how you think and create.

What works:

  • Infographics and diagrams
  • Short video demos or screen recordings
  • GIFs and animations
  • Memes, sketches, hand-drawn visuals
  • Annotated screenshots
  • Charts and data visualizations

File formats: high-resolution PNG, JPG, or GIF for images. Embed video via YouTube or Loom links directly in your doc.

Joel creates the main newsletter thumbnail. Everything inside the post is yours to own visually.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you pay for guest posts?

No. Guest posts are an exchange of audience and authority: your work in front of our 5,000+ leaders, mutual promotion, and republishing rights. You keep full ownership of the content.

Can I include backlinks to my own work?

Yes. Your CTA section can link to your own articles, newsletter, company, or products. Keep it natural and relevant to the piece.

What topics work best?

AI leadership, change management, ethical AI adoption, guardrails, team and org transformation, and decision-making frameworks for leaders navigating AI. Write for executives and managers, not engineers.

Can I use AI to help write my post?

Yes. AI assistance is welcome. But posts that read as fully AI-generated may be declined. Your voice, experience, and point of view are what make a guest post worth running.

Can I republish the post on my own site or newsletter?

Yes. You own the content. You can republish anywhere after it goes live on Leadership in Change.

What happens if my draft needs edits?

Joel may suggest edits for clarity, structure, or audience fit. You'll have a chance to review and approve before publication.

When will my post go live?

Posts are published in the order received. Expect a 2 to 4 week window from accepted submission to publishing date.
