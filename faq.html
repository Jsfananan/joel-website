Coaching, Workshops, or Fractional

Three quick filters. If the work is yours (an individual leader making a decision), start with coaching. If the work is your team's (a rollout, a culture issue, or a skill gap across multiple people), start with a workshop. Fractional CAIO engagements are currently limited and added by referral after a coaching or workshop relationship, so that path opens up once you and Joel are already working together.

Yes for one focused conversation. If the team is the focus though, a team workshop usually does more in three hours than three coaching sessions can. Coaching is built for an individual leader. Workshops are built for groups.

That's how new fractional engagements happen. The coaching or workshop builds the context, and a fractional conversation comes out of it naturally when there's a fit. The context carries forward, so you're never starting from scratch.
