What this engagement is built to remove

Most AI rollouts don't fail because the tool is wrong. They fail because nobody named the real barriers in the team. This engagement is built specifically to find and remove the biggest barriers to successful AI implementation in your company.

The two barriers we focus on

(1) Your team is using AI in ways that quietly atrophy the skills that made them valuable in the first place. (2) Your AI rollout is stalling because the team is hesitating, and pushing harder is making it worse. You pick the focus area that fits your team's situation. The engagement is built around removing that specific barrier.

How the engagement works

Three stages, delivered in sequence. No generic AI training. Built around your team's actual results.

1

Pre-workshop assessment

Every participant takes a short individual assessment. Surfaces where each person actually is on AI use, where their hesitations are, what skills they're worried about losing, and where the real barriers to adoption sit in your specific team. Joel reviews responses confidentially.

2

3-hour live workshop on the team's results

Single session with a break in the middle. Joel walks the team through what the assessment surfaced, addresses the biggest barriers directly, and builds the rule sets and team agreements that come out of that work. Virtual via Google Meet or onsite.

3

Full PDF report with findings + next steps

Delivered within 5 business days. Captures the assessment findings, the decisions made in the room, recommendations tailored to your team, and clear next steps with priorities. This is what your leadership team uses to keep the work moving after the engagement ends.
