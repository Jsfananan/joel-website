So the way I think about it

Most AI rollouts don't fail because the tool is wrong. They fail because the leadership team never named the actual barriers in their team. The hesitation gets ignored. The skill atrophy goes unnamed. The team's real questions never make it into a meeting. By the time anyone notices, the rollout has been stalled for months and nobody can fully say why.

What's included in every engagement

1. Pre-workshop assessment for every participant

A short individual assessment each participant takes before we meet. Surfaces where each person actually is on AI use, where the hesitations live, what skills they're worried about losing, and where the real barriers to implementation sit. Joel reviews the responses confidentially.

2. Custom session prep based on the results

Joel synthesizes the assessment responses, identifies the specific barriers in your team, and builds the session around your team's actual results.

3. 3-hour live workshop on the team's real results

Single block of time with a break in the middle, walking through what the assessment surfaced, addressing the biggest barriers directly with the team in the room, and building the rule set and agreements that come out of that conversation. Online via Google Meet.

4. Full PDF report delivered within 5 business days

Captures the assessment findings, the decisions made in the room, recommendations tailored to your team, and a clear set of next steps with priorities. This is what your leadership team uses to keep the work moving after the engagement ends.

Up to 20 participants
