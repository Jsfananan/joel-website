So the way I think about it

AI is like fire or electricity. World-changing, sure, but just having access to it doesn't make your team better at the work. How you use it does. And the way most teams use it right now is quietly making them smaller. People stop pushing their own thinking because the tool will do it. Other people stop trying altogether because they're not sure if it's safe to even ask the questions out loud.

I built two workshops to address both of those failure modes directly. One protects the thinking your team is built on. The other gives leaders the language and the tools to move people past the quiet hesitation that stalls every AI rollout I've seen.

Both workshops run three hours with a break in the middle. Up to 20 people. Optional pre-workshop assessment for every participant so the session is built around where your team actually is, not where someone assumes you are. Run them separately, or run them back-to-back. Most teams do the second.

The two workshops

Pick the one your team needs first. A lot of organizations end up running both.

Every workshop is built around real practice, not slides. Your team leaves with frameworks they can use the next day, language for the conversations they've been avoiding, and clarity on which AI behaviors are actually helping versus quietly costing them.

What's included in every workshop

3 hours live, one focused session

Single block of time with a break in the middle. Long enough to do real work, short enough to keep the room sharp. Run virtually via Google Meet or onsite at your office.

Up to 20 participants

Full teams, full departments, or small companies. Smaller groups go deeper. Larger groups are quotable separately if you need to bring everyone in.

Optional pre-workshop assessment

A short intake each participant can fill out before the workshop. Surfaces where each person actually is on AI use, where the hesitations live, and what skills they're worried about losing. Results stay confidential. They're used to customize the session.

Custom session prep

Joel meets with the organizer ahead of the workshop to align on goals, the team's current AI state, and the specific decisions you want the team to walk out with.

Practice over slides

Workshops are built around real conversation and exercises tied to your team's actual work. Slides exist when they help. Most of the time they don't.

Post-workshop follow-up

One-page recap with the key frameworks, decisions, and action items your team committed to in the room. Sent within five business days.
