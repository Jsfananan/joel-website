If you searched for a fractional CAIO

This is the same work, without the title

A fractional Chief AI Officer is someone senior who owns the AI question for your organization without being a full-time hire. That is what I do for the companies I work with. They get the strategy, the decisions, the builds, and someone who already knows the business when the next question comes up.

I call it an engagement rather than an officer title because a title implies a seat on your org chart, and what you are actually buying is judgment and hours. If the title matters for how you present it internally, use it. The work is the same either way.
