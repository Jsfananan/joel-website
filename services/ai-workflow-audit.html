AI Workflow Audit

Stop guessing where AI can help. Get a clear roadmap of what to automate and how much you'll save.

Book a Free Discovery Call

The Problem

You know AI can help your business, but you're not sure where to start. You've seen the headlines, tested a few chatbots, maybe even subscribed to ChatGPT Plus. But turning "AI could do this" into "AI is doing this and saving me money" is the gap most leaders struggle to cross.

Without a structured audit, businesses either automate the wrong things, overspend on tools they don't need, or worse: keep bleeding hours on manual tasks that AI could handle in seconds. The average knowledge worker spends 60% of their day on "work about work." That's where the opportunity lives.

The Solution: A Complete AI Workflow Audit

An AI Workflow Audit is a structured analysis of your business operations that identifies the highest-impact opportunities for AI automation. It's not about replacing people; it's about freeing them to do the creative, strategic work that actually moves the needle.

I personally map your workflows, analyze where time and money are being wasted, and deliver a prioritized action plan with custom SOPs you can implement immediately.

What's Included

  • Deep-dive discovery session (90 minutes) to map your current workflows and pain points
  • Complete workflow analysis identifying every task that can be automated or AI-assisted
  • AI opportunity mapping with ROI estimates for each automation opportunity
  • Competitive analysis showing how peers in your industry are using AI
  • 2-3 custom SOPs for your highest-impact tasks, ready to implement
  • Tool recommendations and setup: no guesswork on which AI tools to use
  • Prioritized action plan so you know exactly what to tackle first
  • 30-day optimization support to refine and troubleshoot after implementation

The Process

Week 1: Discovery and mapping. I interview you and key team members, shadow your workflows, and document every process that touches repetitive work.

Week 2: Analysis and design. I identify the top AI opportunities, estimate ROI, and build custom SOPs for your highest-impact tasks.

Weeks 3-4: Delivery and implementation. You receive your complete audit report, action plan, and SOPs. I help set up the recommended tools and provide hands-on training.

Who This Is For

  • Business leaders who know they need AI but don't know where to start
  • Small business owners spending too much time on repetitive tasks
  • Creators and solopreneurs looking to scale without hiring
  • Teams that have tried AI tools but aren't seeing real results

Expected Results

Clients typically save $1,500-3,000/month in time value after implementing their AI Workflow Audit recommendations. Most see measurable ROI within the first 30 days.

Why Work With Me

I don't sell software or take commissions on tool recommendations. My only incentive is finding the best solution for your business. I've conducted workflow audits for solopreneurs, nonprofit teams, and growing businesses; and I use AI daily to run my own three companies.
