An AI workflow audit maps the processes in your organization where AI can save the most time and reduce the most friction. For most small businesses and nonprofits, this surfaces one or two clear priorities and a practical path forward. The audit often becomes the starting point for a coaching engagement or a focused marketing-tool build, depending on what the organization needs most.

AI Workflow Audit

Stop guessing where AI can help. Get a clear map of what to improve and a practical path forward.

Book a Free Discovery Call

The way I think about an AI workflow audit is this: most organizations that come to me have already tried a few AI tools, and the tools mostly work, but the results feel underwhelming. What's usually missing is clarity on which workflows actually benefit from AI and which ones aren't worth the disruption. A workflow audit maps that out. We look at where your team's time goes, which processes have predictable inputs and outputs, and where the friction is highest. What comes out the other side is usually one or two clear priorities and a practical path forward, whether that's a coaching engagement to work through the strategy, or a specific build to solve the most painful problem. It's a starting point, not a standalone deliverable.

Use Case Examples

  • A marketing team spending 12 hours per week on content research and formatting, most of which was repetitive enough to be automated with a well-configured tool.
  • An operations director at a nonprofit who couldn't get clear visibility on which AI tools her team was already using informally, and needed a map before building any governance policy.

The Process

Week 1: Discovery and mapping. I interview you and key team members, shadow your workflows, and document every process that touches repetitive work.

Week 2: Analysis and design. I identify the top AI opportunities, estimate ROI, and build custom SOPs for your highest-impact tasks.

Weeks 3-4: Delivery and implementation. You receive your complete audit report, action plan, and SOPs. I help set up the recommended tools and provide hands-on training.
