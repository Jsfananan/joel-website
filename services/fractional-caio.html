Three options. Only one works for small and mid-sized teams.

Every board is asking about AI. Most small and mid-sized organizations are stuck choosing between two bad answers, when a third option quietly outperforms both.

Option 1: Hire a full-time Chief AI Officer.

$250,000 to $400,000 a year, plus benefits and equity, plus a six to nine month recruit. Out of reach for most organizations, and overkill for most workloads.

Option 2: Do nothing and hope.

Tools get purchased without a strategy. Pilots stall. Budget burns on software nobody adopts. That is a gamble, not an investment.

Option 3: Fractional AI leadership.

Around ten percent of the cost of a full-time hire, measurable wins in 30 to 45 days instead of six to nine months, and strategic AI leadership that scales to what your organization can actually absorb.

You don't need a $250,000 hire. You need a plan.

Most small and mid-sized businesses and nonprofits can't justify a full-time Chief AI Officer, and most don't need one. What you actually need is the strategic thinking, the governance, and a roadmap you can act on.

Frequently asked questions

A fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) is an experienced AI leader who owns your AI strategy, governance, and roadmap on a part-time monthly retainer, at roughly 10% of the cost of a full-time hire. Your company likely needs one if your board is asking about AI and you don't have a clean answer, your team is adopting tools without a strategy (shadow AI), or you can feel competitors pulling ahead. You probably don't need one if you have a single, well-scoped project. In that case, a coaching session or a team workshop is the better first step.

The Advisor tier ($1,750/mo) is built for small businesses with 10 to 100 employees. The Operator tier ($3,500/mo) is designed for small to mid-sized businesses with 50 to 500 employees. If you're larger and need more hours, we can discuss a custom retainer.

Consultants drop in, deliver a report, and leave. A fractional CAIO is embedded in your strategy. You get continuity, accountability, and someone who knows your business over time. I also build alongside you when it makes sense, not just advise.
