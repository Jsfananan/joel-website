Fractional Chief AI Officer

AI leadership for small and mid-sized businesses that need a clear plan, not a $250,000 hire.

Book a Free Discovery Call See Packages

You don't need a $250,000 hire. You need a plan.

Most small and mid-sized businesses can't justify a full-time Chief AI Officer, and most don't need one. What you actually need is the strategic thinking, the governance, and a roadmap you can act on. The real risk isn't what you spend on a fractional CAIO. It's what you'll waste without one when AI tools get purchased, pilots stall, and nothing meaningful ships.

My philosophy: one process at a time, humans at the center

Start small, prove the win, build from there

I don't try to overhaul a company with AI. We find one process that can be improved, give your team meaningful time back, and build confidence before moving to the next thing.

Human expertise comes first

I only work with companies that value human work. If the goal is to automate your people out of a job, I'm not the right partner. If the goal is to amplify what your team already does well, let's talk.

You're probably here because one of these is true

Your board is asking about AI and you don't have a clean answer.

Directors assume you have a strategy in place. You don't, or it's scattered across teams with no alignment.

Your team is experimenting with tools and it's starting to get messy.

Shadow AI is creating risk. Compliance questions aren't being asked. Duplicate tools are being purchased.

Your competitors are moving and you can feel the gap opening.

They're shipping AI-powered workflows. You're still in discovery mode while the market is accelerating.

What the first 90 days look like

Days 1 to 30: Discovery and alignment

We map your current AI activity, identify shadow AI, review governance gaps, and find the first process worth tackling. You get clarity on where you stand and a plan for what to do about it.

Days 31 to 60: Design and roadmap

We draft your governance framework, design the change management plan for your first priority process, and get your team aligned before anything touches production.

Days 61 to 90: First pilot in motion

We move your first priority process forward together. What we deliver by day 90 depends on the scope and your team's capacity, and we define that together during discovery.
