How to evaluate a fractional CAIO

The fractional AI leadership market is new, and the quality spread is wide. Before you hire anyone (including me), make sure they clear these five bars.

1. Leads with strategy, not tools

A real fractional CAIO starts with your business outcomes and works backward to the tools. If the first conversation is about a specific vendor, it's the wrong conversation.

2. Deep change management expertise

AI projects fail on adoption, not on technology. Your fractional leader needs real experience leading people through change, not just slides about it.

3. Recent hands-on shipping experience

The person advising you should have shipped real AI work in the last twelve months. The landscape moves too fast for someone who hasn't touched a model since 2023.

4. A reasonable client cap

If they have forty clients, you're getting a fraction of a fraction. Ask how many active engagements they carry. Mine is capped at fourteen.

5. Amplification, not replacement

The right partner is focused on making your team more valuable, not on automating them out. If the pitch is headcount reduction, find someone else.
