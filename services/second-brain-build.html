The Problem

You read articles, attend conferences, listen to podcasts, and have conversations full of valuable insights. But when you need that one idea three weeks later, it's gone. Your notes are scattered across apps, your bookmarks are a graveyard, and your browser tabs multiply like rabbits.

You're not lacking information. You're lacking a system to capture, organize, and retrieve knowledge when it matters most. Without one, you waste hours re-researching things you've already learned and miss connections between ideas that could transform your work.

The Solution: Your AI-Powered Second Brain

A Second Brain is a personal knowledge management system that stores, organizes, and surfaces your most valuable information using AI. Think of it as an external hard drive for your mind, powered by tools like Claude Projects and ChatGPT, designed to match how you actually think and work.

Unlike generic note-taking apps, your Second Brain uses AI to connect ideas across domains, generate insights from your stored knowledge, and help you create content, make decisions, and solve problems faster than ever.

  • Claude Projects and ChatGPT configuration with optimized prompts and knowledge bases

  • Two 60-minute 1-on-1 training sessions to master your new system

The Process

Week 1: Discovery call and workflow assessment. I learn how you work, what tools you already use, and where knowledge falls through the cracks.

Week 2: I build your custom Second Brain architecture, configure your AI tools, and migrate your existing content.

Who This Is For
