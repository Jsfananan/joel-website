Second Brain Build

Give AI the context it needs to give you genuinely useful answers, not generic ones.

Book a Free Discovery Call

A second brain build is, honestly, the most common first build I do with executives and CEOs. The problem it solves is pretty specific: you have years of experience, documents, decisions, and context in your head and scattered across tools, and every time you ask AI a question, you get a generic answer because you haven't given it any of that context. A second brain system changes that. It's a custom AI knowledge management setup configured around how you actually work, pulling in your key documents and decisions so that when you ask AI a question, it's answering from your actual context, not the internet. This is frequently built as part of a coaching engagement rather than a standalone project, because getting the configuration right requires understanding how you think and work before we build anything.

Use Case Examples

  • A CEO running four businesses who needed each one to have its own AI context loaded, so that when he was working in one business, AI wasn't pulling in assumptions from another.
  • An executive director at a nonprofit who wanted AI to be able to answer questions about their grant history, program structure, and donor relationships without her having to re-explain the organization every session.

How this fits.

Most Second Brain builds happen as part of a coaching engagement or fractional retainer. Standalone builds are available too. Either way, scoped within the $2,000 to $5,000 build range.
