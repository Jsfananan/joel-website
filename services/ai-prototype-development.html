The Problem

You have a great idea for an AI-powered tool or product, but you're stuck. Maybe you've tried describing it to developers and gotten back proposals that miss the mark. Maybe you've quoted out a full build and the price tag makes your eyes water. Or maybe you're just not sure the idea will work at all.

Building an AI product without a prototype is like writing a book without an outline. You end up spending months and thousands of dollars building the wrong thing. A working prototype lets you test, iterate, and validate before you commit.

The Solution: Idea to Prototype

I take your AI concept and turn it into a working, interactive prototype you can test with real users. This isn't a mockup or a slide deck — it's a functional tool that demonstrates your core value proposition and proves your idea works.

Whether you need a custom GPT, an AI-powered workflow, a chatbot, or an internal tool, I handle the full development cycle from concept through delivery.

What's Included

  • Concept refinement session to clarify your idea, target user, and success criteria
  • Technical architecture design choosing the right AI models, APIs, and tools for your use case
  • End-to-end prototype development with working AI integration
  • User interface design that's clean, intuitive, and ready for user testing
  • Two rounds of iteration based on your feedback and testing results
  • Documentation and handoff so you can continue development independently or with a team
  • 30-day support for questions and minor adjustments post-delivery

The Process

Phase 1 - Discovery: We define the problem, the user, and the core features. I research the best technical approach and present a plan.

Phase 2 - Build: I develop the prototype, integrating AI capabilities and building the interface. You get progress updates and can provide feedback along the way.

Phase 3 - Test and Iterate: You test the prototype. I refine based on feedback. Two rounds of iteration are included to get it right.

Phase 4 - Deliver: You receive the working prototype, documentation, and a roadmap for next steps if you want to take it to production.

Who This Is For

  • Entrepreneurs with AI product ideas who need to validate before investing
  • Business leaders who want a custom AI tool built for their specific workflow
  • Creators who want to offer AI-powered experiences to their audience
  • Teams that need a proof-of-concept to get stakeholder buy-in

Expected Results

A working prototype that you can demo, test with users, and use to make informed decisions about full development. Clients use prototypes to secure funding, validate market demand, and save months of wasted development time.
