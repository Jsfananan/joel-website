The Problem

AI is reshaping every industry, and leaders are expected to have an AI strategy. But most executives didn't sign up to become technologists. You're navigating board questions about AI, vendor pitches that all sound the same, and team anxiety about job displacement, all while trying to run your actual business.

The real risk isn't that AI will replace leaders. It's that leaders who don't understand AI strategically will fall behind those who do. You don't need to code. You need to lead.

The Solution: 1-on-1 Executive AI Coaching

Executive AI Coaching is a structured, ongoing partnership where you get strategic guidance tailored to your specific leadership challenges. Each session is a focused 60-minute conversation designed to help you make better decisions, faster, about AI in your organization.

This is personalized, one-on-one coaching tailored to your specific AI challenges. It meets you where you are and adapts to your goals, your industry, and your pace.

AI Strategy Coaching for Founders

If you're a founder or CEO building a company in the AI era, the questions are different. Where do we bet? What do we build versus buy? Which workflows should be AI-native from day one? How do we hire for an AI-augmented team? I work with founders 1-on-1 to translate AI capability into business strategy, not just tool selection. You ship faster because you're making the right calls earlier.

  • AI strategy development: build a clear, actionable AI roadmap for your organization
  • Change management: lead your team through AI adoption without resistance or burnout

  • Ethical AI governance: create responsible AI policies that protect your reputation

How It Works

Free discovery call: We meet for 30 minutes to understand your current situation, challenges, and goals. No pressure, no pitch.

Coaching sessions: 60-minute video calls on a schedule that works for you: weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Each session has a clear agenda and actionable takeaways.

Between sessions: You get access to resources, frameworks, and email support to keep momentum between calls.
