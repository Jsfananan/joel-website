## 1. The Thesis

The Chief AI Officer role is the fastest-growing C-suite title in history (70% YoY growth). Every board is asking about AI. A full-time Chief AI Officer runs around **$250,000/year** — plus benefits, equity, and a 6-month recruit. Most small and mid-sized businesses don't have that kind of budget sitting around. **They don't need to.** 70% of executives say they need outside help to extract AI value (RSM/Deloitte 2026). The fractional model is how that gap gets filled — and the market is still forming (Faye launched March 17, 2026; no dominant player owns SEO or the "change management + builder" angle yet).

**This is the flagship offer on jsalinas.org.** Joel also does custom AI builds and one-off coaching sessions, but the Fractional CAIO service is the bread and butter — the primary revenue engine and the offer every website visitor should see first.

**Joel's unfair advantage:**
1. **Change management background** (Sr Director of Growth Marketing, humanitarian sector) — every competitor is pure strategy or pure tech; nobody leads with people-side transformation
2. **Leadership in Change newsletter** — built-in top-of-funnel and trust engine
3. **Hands-on builder** — actually ships AI tools, not slide decks
4. **Thought leadership platform** — LiC articles prove authority before the sales call
5. **Bilingual (English + Spanish)** — US-based, serving clients across both markets
6. **100% remote** — Google Meet, async-first, no travel overhead baked into the price

**Positioning line:**
> *Fractional Chief AI Officer for small and mid-sized businesses that need AI leadership, not another consultant. Strategy. Hands-on builds. Change management. No six-month discovery. Measurable wins in 90 days.*

**The one sentence that does all the work:**
> *Most small and mid-sized businesses don't have $250,000 to spend on a Chief AI Officer. They don't need to. You need a CAIO's brain, not their salary.*

**The ROI argument (use this everywhere):**
> *The real risk isn't the cost of a fractional CAIO — it's the cost of NOT having one. A typical mid-market AI implementation runs $50,000 or more. Without a clear implementation strategy tied to business outcomes, that money gets burned on tools nobody uses, pilots that never ship, and governance gaps that expose your data. A fractional CAIO is the cheapest insurance policy you can buy against a failed AI investment. Strategy first. Then tools. Then results.*

## 2. The Offer

All meetings remote via Google Meet. US-based, English + Spanish.

### Tier 1 — Advisor — $1,500/month
- Up to **2 hours/month** live strategy (remote, Google Meet)
- **Unlimited async email + Slack + Loom** (24-hr response, business hours)
- **Quarterly** AI opportunity scan (2–3 highest-ROI use cases)
- Vendor/tool vetting library access
- Quarterly exec team check-in **as needed**
- **For:** Small businesses, 10–100 employees, boards asking "what's our AI plan?"

### Tier 2 — Operator — $3,000/month
- Up to **6 hours/month** live strategy (remote)
- **Unlimited async** (same SLA)
- **Quarterly** AI opportunity scan + prioritized roadmap
- **1 exec workshop/quarter** (60–90 min)
- **Board-ready AI update deck/quarter**
- Change management playbook per rollout
- Shadow AI / governance framework
- Quarterly exec team check-in as needed
- **For:** Small to mid-sized businesses, 50–500 employees, board pressure to show ROI

### Build Add-Ons — $2,000–$5,000/build
- Custom AI tool, agent, workflow, or integration
- Scoped per project, delivered in 2–4 weeks
- Sold separately from retainer; available to any client

### Future Tier 3 — Embedded — $5,000/month *(design now, launch by Day 90)*
- 10+ hours/month live, priority access, embedded in leadership cadence
- **Bundle of 3 builds included** (or credited)
- For Operator clients who outgrow their tier

### Other offerings on jsalinas.org (supporting, not flagship)
- **Custom AI builds** — $2K–$5K+ per build (also available as retainer add-on)
- **One-off coaching sessions** — pay-per-session for leaders who want a single strategy conversation

### Rules
- **Unlimited email = async-only, 24-hr response.** State it clearly.
- **6-month minimum commitment.** Month-to-month available for **+$500/month**.
- **All meetings remote.** Google Meet default.
- **Capacity caps:** 8 Advisor + 4 Operator + 2 Embedded max. Scarcity is real.
